🚀 **I built a full-stack subscription billing platform — and I'm proud of it.**

Meet **BillFlow** — a complete SaaS billing system I built from scratch as my final year project.

What started as "just a project" turned into the most challenging and rewarding experience of my developer journey.

**What it does:**
→ Real-time dashboard with MRR, ARR, and revenue analytics
→ Customer management with search and detailed profiles
→ Subscription lifecycle tracking (active, trial, past due, paused, canceled)
→ Invoice generation and payment history
→ Failed payment recovery (dunning) system
→ Coupon and discount code management
→ Dark/light theme with smooth animations

**Tech stack:**
Next.js 16 • TypeScript • Express.js • Prisma • PostgreSQL • Tailwind CSS • Framer Motion

**What made it special:**
This wasn't just about writing code. It was about understanding how real businesses work — how revenue flows, how subscriptions evolve, how failed payments can be recovered, and how data needs to be presented in a way that actually means something.

The hardest part? Getting the MRR calculation right. Revenue isn't just "add up the payments" — you need to account for plan changes, refunds, trial periods, and mid-cycle upgrades.

The most satisfying part? Watching the dashboard come alive with real data — seeing the charts move, the numbers update, and the whole system work together seamlessly.

**Biggest lesson:**
Good software isn't about fancy algorithms. It's about solving real problems in a way that feels effortless to the user.

Grateful for every late night, every bug that taught me something, and every small win that kept me going.
